What it does

Built around how advocates actually work

01

Watch your case move, live

The Live Display Board mirrors real-time court proceedings, so you can see exactly when your case is called — without sitting glued to a courtroom screen.

02

Today's cause list, already on your phone

Daily and weekly cause lists are fetched automatically — no more manually searching court websites every morning before you head out.

03

Know the moment something changes

Smart Case Tracking watches your matters for status updates and sends instant notifications, so you're never the last to know.

04

Find any case in seconds

Advanced Search lets you pull up older cases using multiple criteria, turning a five-minute hunt into a five-second lookup.

05

Orders and judgments, one tap away

Review full case details and download or read PDF orders and judgments directly inside the app — no inbox digging, no separate downloads folder.

06

Plan more than just today

Multi-Date Cause Lists let you check listings across different dates, so you can prepare for the rest of the week, not just the morning ahead.

07

Organized the way courts work

Case Sorting lets you order your matters by court number, mirroring the way listings actually run on the ground.
